# FairWork — Product Specification

## Vision
A local-first home-services marketplace for Denton County homeowners and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) Pros where ranking is earned through completed work and verified reviews—never ad spend or lead fees.

## Core Requirements
1. **Local Self-Hosted Stack:** Web frontend, backend API, database, object storage, and automated tests must run locally via Docker Compose.
2. **Two-Sided Accounts:** Homeowners (Denton County service area) and Pros (DFW metroplex).
3. **Seven Launch Services:** Pressure washing, window cleaning, junk removal, drywall patch/repair, knob/lock install/replace, tree/bush trimming, and gutter cleaning.
4. **Pro Credential Verification:** Pros must upload a Certificate of Insurance (COI) and business registration (or government photo ID for sole proprietors) before bidding.
5. **Zero Lead Fees & Quote Comparison:** Homeowners post a job once; verified Pros submit quotes free; platform fee applies only when a job completes.
6. **Merit-Based Ranking:** Pros rank strictly by completed jobs, review quality, and reliability—no paid placement.
7. **Verified Reviews Only:** Reviews can only be submitted after a job is marked completed and paid.
